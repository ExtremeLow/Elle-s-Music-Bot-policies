Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who operates the bot

This notice describes the processing of personal data by All Seeing Eye, a Discord music bot operated by extreme_low. Privacy contact: [extreme12low@gmail.com].

Saved playlist data

The playlist database stores your Discord user ID, playlist names, song titles, and song page URLs. These records let you manage personal playlists and use them in different servers with this bot. The current playlist tables do not intentionally store your email address, password, payment details, or a recording of voice conversations.

Data used while the bot runs

The bot processes Discord server, channel, message, and user identifiers; display names and avatar references used in panels; voice-channel membership; submitted song queries and URLs; playback queues; the current and previous song; volume; and loop settings. Much of this state is held in memory. Clearing a queue or leaving voice clears queued tracks, but some state, including a previous-song reference, can remain in memory until the bot restarts.

Channel history and panels

When panel cleanup is enabled, the bot examines up to the most recent 100 messages in a channel when a new recognised panel appears. It checks authors, embed information, and pinned status to identify its own obsolete panels. This cleanup code does not intentionally copy channel history into a database. The bot retains references to active panels in memory so it can update or delete them.

Logs

Operational output can contain connection information, server or channel identifiers, song titles, source URLs, and error details. Some media URLs may contain the bot host’s public IP address. Hosting services or console tools may retain this output. Log and backup retention: All logs are not saved but your playlists.

Why data is used

Data is used to provide requested searches and music playback, save and retrieve playlists, display and refresh panels, apply voice-channel controls, remove obsolete bot panels, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service. It is not necessary to supply an email address to use the implemented music commands.

Lawful basis where applicable

Where data-protection law requires a lawful basis, processing necessary to deliver the service you request is based on performance of that service agreement; proportionate security and troubleshooting processing is based on legitimate interests; and processing required by law is based on legal obligations. Any future optional processing requiring consent will be explained separately. Applicable local rights and requirements remain in effect.

Who can see information

Other users can hear requested audio in shared voice channels. Depending on Discord permissions, they can also see song titles, links, queue entries, panel requester names or mentions, and voice-channel statuses. Listing or editing personal playlists is designed to be limited to the requesting account through the bot’s commands, but playing a playlist displays information about it in the server.

Third parties and hosting

Discord receives and processes bot interactions, messages, and voice connections under its own policies. Queries and media requests are sent to the selected source, such as YouTube, to resolve or play the audio; those services receive the request and network information needed to respond. The operator and any hosting provider may access data needed to operate and secure the bot. Bot hosting provider and processing locations: Selfhosted. Information may be processed outside your country. Any legally required international-transfer safeguards must be identified here by the operator before publication. The implemented bot does not include advertising, data-sale, or tracking-analytics features.

Retention and deletion

Personal playlists remain in the database until you delete them or the operator removes them. Use /playlist delete name:YOUR_PLAYLIST_NAME to remove a playlist and its associated track records from the active database. Removing the bot from a server does not delete your playlists. For deletion of all retained personal data, contact the operator with your Discord user ID; you may be asked to verify account ownership. Deleted data can remain in backups until the stated backup retention period ends. Existing Discord messages and source-service records are governed by their own retention and deletion controls. This notice does not promise deletion from third-party systems the operator does not control.

Your choices and rights

You can stop using the bot, manage or delete playlists through its commands, and ask the operator about access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objections where applicable. Requests: extreme12low@gmail.com. The operator will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may complain to your local data-protection authority; UK users can contact the Information Commissioner’s Office at https://ico.org.uk/ .

Security and voice audio

The bot does not intentionally record conversations or store microphone audio. It connects to voice to play requested audio. The operator must protect the bot token, database, hosting account, and any backups with appropriate access controls. No service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit secrets in commands or playlist names.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the bot’s processing changes. Check the revision date for the current version. Material changes will be announced through the published support contact or community where available.
